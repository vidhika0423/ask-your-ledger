MASTER SERVICE AGREEMENT
Between: Acme Corp ("Vendor") and Northwind Trading ("Customer")
Effective Date: January 15, 2024

SECTION 1 — PAYMENT TERMS
Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date ("net-30"). Invoices
not paid within this period are considered overdue as of the day
following the due date.

SECTION 2 — LATE PAYMENT PENALTY
Late payments incur a penalty of 1.5% per month on the outstanding
balance, calculated from the due date until the date of payment.
Penalties compound monthly and are added to the next invoice cycle.

SECTION 3 — CONTRACT RENEWAL
This agreement renews automatically each year on the anniversary of
the Effective Date unless either party provides written notice of
non-renewal at least 90 days prior to the renewal date.

SECTION 4 — TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days written notice.
If termination occurs within 30 days of a scheduled renewal, any
volume discount applied for the upcoming term is forfeited, and
outstanding late payment penalties under Section 2 remain payable
regardless of termination.

SECTION 5 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any invoice dispute must be raised in writing within 15 days of the
invoice date. Disputes raised after this window are not eligible for
adjustment, though the payment terms in Section 1 still apply to the
undisputed portion of the invoice.
